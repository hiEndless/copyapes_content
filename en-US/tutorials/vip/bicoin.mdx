<Note>
  Bicoin’s API is less stable than exchange APIs, and latency varies by trader and account. It may be zero delay, or anywhere from a few seconds to several minutes.
</Note>

<Step>

  <StepItem title="Select Bicoin copy trading">
    Enter your Bicoin account and password.

    If the person you want to copy has enabled paid subscription on Bicoin, you must first obtain their subscription code before you can copy.

    <img
      src="@asset:tutorials/bc.png"

    />
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Search for the target trader">
    <img
      src="@asset:tutorials/bc1.png"
      alt="Bc1 Pn"
      title="Bc1 Pn"
      style={{ width:"72%" }}
    />

    Select the trader account you want to copy.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Choose a copy mode">
    Two modes are available: copy from operation history, and copy from futures positions.

    <Note>
      Operation-history copy is faster than futures-position copy\
      Futures-position copy is more accurate than operation-history copy
    </Note>
    Because spot trades also appear in the operation history and can interfere with copying, you can contact support to set a trader whitelist and actively filter that trader’s spot operations.
  </StepItem>

</Step>
