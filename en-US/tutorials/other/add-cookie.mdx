There are two ways to add a cookie: via the extension, or manually.

We recommend using the browser extension provided by CopyApes.

<Icon icon="star" color="#ff0000" />Extension download and illustrated guide→[**View**](https://xwvmohge80.feishu.cn/docx/OWvbdwKKvo4qpXxRVOAcg40mnub)

## Extension cookie video tutorial

<Note>
  After logging into the extension in your browser, each time you refresh the trader’s lead-trading page, a cookie is fetched automatically. If you click Refresh Data without being logged into the exchange, you will get an invalid cookie.\
  \
  When updating a cookie while cookie copy-trading tasks are running, we recommend re-logging into the exchange account first, then opening the trader’s lead-trading page and refreshing via the CopyApes extension. This allows a seamless online sync update without interrupting active copy-trading tasks.\
  After logging out of the exchange, you must refresh the cookie within 5 minutes; otherwise the copy-trading task will stop because it cannot crawl valid data.
</Note>
